# catavasia roadmap (user requirements, 2026-10-05)

Design research and evidence: [orchestration-spec.md](orchestration-spec.md).

## Integration step (after feat/idle-activities, feat/cat-playroom, feat/cat-social, feat/pet-care land)

1. Wire the social layer (talk, joint play, fights) into the idle-activity registry: coffee spot, playroom toys.
2. Pets join the idle pool: playroom toys, sofa sleep, fights. Pets never drink coffee.
3. Furniture: pet beds (round cushion, basket, radiator hammock, donut blanket) and cat houses (cardboard house with window, felt igloo cave, wooden house with roof, two-storey condo). Sleeping inside shows only ears or tail. Pets get a new need "sleep/energy" and walk to the nearest free bed/house. Agent cats may sleep there too, as an idle variant.
4. Spot reservation (requirement): a cat (agent or pet) reserves a spot (seat, bed, house, toy, bowl) when it STARTS walking to it. No other cat may target a reserved or occupied spot.
5. Contention (requirement): when two cats claim the same spot at the same moment (idle choice or need), there is a probability of a fight, using the social-layer fight scene. The loser picks another spot.

### Integration decisions (feat/integration, 1.4.1-cats.9, agent-made while the user slept)

- One service, `engine/spotReservations.ts`, keyed by tile. `engine/spotClaims.ts` wraps it for the office; `engine/catLife.ts` wires it to idle activities, pet care (bowls, litter box, provider claims), joint play and seat assignment.
- A cat reserves at walk start. A per-frame reconcile releases every key its holder no longer wants (arrived then left, interrupted, scene over), drops keys of despawned holders, and syncs assigned seats in as `seat` reservations.
- A seat outranks a spot: when the user seats an agent where a cat naps, the cat gives way. A new agent never gets a sofa seat a cat holds.
- Contest = a claim on a spot another cat reserved less than `SPOT_CONTEST_WINDOW_SEC` (1.5 s) ago and has not reached. The roll is `SPOT_CONTEST_FIGHT_CHANCE` (0.35) for a fight, winner 50/50; otherwise the second cat re-picks. No fight beyond `SPOT_CONTEST_FIGHT_RADIUS_TILES` (8) or inside the pair's social cooldown: the second cat re-picks.
- The fight winner holds the spot through the scene and walks back to it when the scene ends; the loser picks something else.
- Pets join social scenes through stand-in actor characters (`engine/petActors.ts`, ids from `PET_ACTOR_ID_BASE`). Pets have no talk/angry art: they show the pictogram bubbles, anger marks and the dust cloud over their own sprite. Only cat pets take part.
- Pets idle-play with yarn, mouse, teaser and scratching post (hop pose); they nap on beds, in houses, on the cat bed, on sofa seats or the floor by a sofa. No box, cat tree or tunnel for pets (agent-only poses). Pets never get a coffee claim.
- Energy: 6th need, decays 8/office-hour while awake, refills 2.5/real-second asleep, wakes early when full. Under 35 a pet seeks the nearest free bed/house, then sofa, then floor. Energy raises no request bubble. Old `pets-state.json` loads with fresh energy (no version bump).
- Activity social: every 2 s, chance 0.35, the closest pair at coffee or in the playroom talks in place; in the playroom 60 % of those become joint play: turns at a yarn ball, a mouse or a teaser, a chase when one of them is at the tunnel.
- Furniture: new "Cat beds" catalog tab, 4 beds and 4 houses generated by `scripts/generate-bed-sprites.mjs` (MIT). Inside a house only ears (cardboard, condo) or a tail (igloo, wooden) show, recolored with the cat's fur; near-black cats get a light rim.
- Known limits: `npm install -g` swaps the running server's files, so a lazily loaded module could fail in the old server during the short time before the restart; joint play on a one-sided toy (teaser) puts the second cat behind the first; pet idle frames face the viewer, so a waiting pet in joint play looks at the camera; reservations cover activity spots, not tiles where a cat merely stands.

## Orchestration (spec: orchestration-spec.md). User decisions

- Engine: own orchestrator. Each cat is a long-lived `claude -p` stream-json session owned by the catavasia server. An HTTP MCP server in Fastify gives the tools brief, delegate, report, ask, reply, list_team. The server enforces the hierarchy.
- Worktrees: one worktree per worker cat. The boss merges the workers' branches into the task branch.
- Models: boss Opus, workers Sonnet, narrator Haiku 4.5. Each can be changed per cat.
- Lifecycle: sessions run on demand, sleep after 1 hour of idle, and resume. Rationale (user): by then the prompt cache has expired anyway, so a live process gives no saving. Tie the threshold to the session's real cache TTL: read it from the stream-json usage fields (ephemeral 5m vs 1h cache creation) and verify. Default 1 h as a constant with --resume. The cat stays visible in the game.
- Defaults I chose: ask goes up/down the tree plus siblings. The narrator batches every 10 s. Permissions are skipped, as for tasks. Reuse upstream PR #347 (PTY + xterm.js) in the fork.
- Terminal: a chat console by default. "Take the wheel" opens a real `claude --resume` PTY when the cat is idle. A per-cat lock prevents two processes on one session.
- Narrator: superseded, see the RAM plan item 3 (templates + batched one-shot Haiku calls). Short English text. The server validates its output against a schema.
- Office shows ONLY own cats from the Cats menu. External claude sessions appear as guests, behind a Settings toggle that is OFF by default.
- One session = one cat. Subagents are not shown (fix/one-cat-per-session in progress).
- The Cats menu: Agents tab (look, name, prompt, role, model, effort: passed as `--model` / `--effort`; list the values the installed `claude --help` accepts, do not hardcode from memory), Hierarchy tab (tree editor), Pets tab (create, name, colour/pattern from the shared editor with more colours).
- A task flows boss → briefing meeting (all cats gather in one room) → delegation down → work → reports up → result to the user. Cats walk to each other for every conversation. Speech bubbles appear, and hover shows the narrator summary.

### Cat terminal decisions (feat/cat-terminal, phase 2b, agent-made)

- Click a cat, then `>_` on its selected label: a docked panel opens (Chat tab default, Terminal tab). The task-modal click on a finished task cat stays unchanged.
- Client contract `webview-ui/src/catTerminal/catSessionApi.ts`: `subscribe(catId, onEvent, onGone?)`, `send(catId, text)`, `canTakeWheel(catId)`, `takeWheel(catId, size, handlers)`, `releaseWheel(catId)`. Wire frames live in `core/src/catSession.ts`.
- Server endpoints (`server/src/catTerminal/`): WS `/api/cat-sessions/:catId/events` (snapshot, then `entries` and `status` frames; same-origin only), POST `/api/cat-sessions/:catId/messages` `{text}` (token), WS `/api/cat-sessions/:catId/terminal?token=&cols=&rows=` (token + same-origin).
- Phase 1 plugs in by implementing `CatSessionSource` (`snapshot`, `subscribe`, `send`, `beginWheel`, `endWheel`) and passing it where `httpServer.ts` builds `TaskBoardCatSource`. Today `catId` is the office agent id of a task-board cat. Phase 1 may use its own cat ids.
- Lock: `catTerminal/sessionLocks.ts` holds one owner (`turn` or `wheel`) per session id. Phase 1 MUST take this same lock before it spawns a turn. A message or the wheel during a running turn gets HTTP 409 / WS close 4409.
- A message to a task cat is one more headless turn: `claude --resume <id> -p` in the task worktree (checked out again from `task/<id>`), committed again when it ends. No mid-turn injection yet: the `priority: "now"` stream-json input needs phase 1's long-lived stdin. Streaming = rows arrive live per stream-json record, no `--include-partial-messages` token deltas.
- The wheel: `claude --resume <id>` (interactive, normal permission prompts) in a PTY with cwd = the session's worktree. The PTY lives as long as its socket: closing the tab or "Give it back" kills it, commits the worktree and frees the lock. No headless-xterm replay on reload (upstream PR #347 has it; not needed for a one-socket drawer).
- node-pty: `@lydell/node-pty` (optional dependency, prebuilt, verified to spawn on this Mac, Node 24). Loader ported from PR #347 `ptyModule.ts`. Without a working module the Terminal tab shows the reason and the chat still works.
- Known limits: StrictMode double-mount in `vite dev` takes the wheel twice (the second gets 409). Cats without a task session show "no session" in the panel.

### Cats menu UI (phase 2a, feat/cats-menu-ui, agent decisions 2026-10-06)

- The toolbar has **Cats** (tabs Agents, Pets) and **Hierarchy** buttons. The Agents tab and Hierarchy talk to the server since phase 3 (see "Wiring decisions"). The Pets tab edits the real office pets (see "Pets tab" below).
- The agent UI talks only to `webview-ui/src/cats/catsApi.ts` (`CatsApi`). Today `localCatsAdapter.ts` implements it in memory and mirrors it to `localStorage` key `catavasia.cats.v1`. Phase 1 had pushed no `core` types when this landed, so the names are ours.
- Types: `CatProfile {id, name, appearance, role, systemPrompt, engine: 'claude'|'codex', model, effort, parentId: string|null, isDefault?}`, `Appearance {breed?, colors?: {fur, belly, stripe, patchA, patchB, point}, pattern?, eyes?, collar?: hex|'none'}`. Colours are `#rrggbb`. Breed ids are the lower-case breed names (`marmalade` … `bear`).
- Methods: `getSnapshot()`, `subscribe()`, `saveCat()`, `deleteCat()`, `setParent(id, parentId)`, `promoteToBoss(id)`, `engineOptions(engine)`. Mutations are fire-and-forget. The new state arrives through `subscribe`, so a transport adapter can apply them after a server round trip.
- Phase 1 mapping (thin adapter): `catProfilesLoaded` / `catProfileSaved` → replace the snapshot and notify. `hierarchy` → set `parentId` on each cat. `saveCat`, `deleteCat`, `setParent`, `promoteToBoss` → the matching client messages. `CatProfile.persona` in the spec = `systemPrompt` here. `engineOptions` must come from the server (it reads `claude --help` and `codex debug models`). The static lists in the local adapter are offline fallbacks only.
- Hierarchy rule: exactly one boss (`parentId === null`). The server must enforce the same rules as `cats/hierarchy.ts`: no cycles, a deleted cat's reports move up, a deleted boss hands over to its first report, "promote to boss" puts the old boss under the new one.
- Sprites: the webview reuses `scripts/cats/breeds.mjs` + `poses.mjs` directly (typed by `.d.mts` files), so custom looks render at runtime with no server round trip. `cats/catArt.ts` turns an `Appearance` into a breed object. 13 breed presets plus 18 coat presets for both agents and pets.
- Delete uses an inline confirm button: VS Code webviews block `window.confirm`.

#### Pets tab = the pet cats in the layout (feat/pets-tab-real-pets, agent decisions 2026-10-06)

Pets stay in the layout data; Cats → Pets adds, renames, recolours and deletes them.
- **Where the look and name live (decided):** on the layout entry. `PlacedPet {id, petType, name?, appearance?: Appearance}`. Both fields are optional. A Gitcat entry without them stays `{id, petType}` and renders exactly as before. A bad name or coat in a hand-edited file falls back to the manifest name / the template sheet. No separate pets profile file: one file, one save path.
- **Save path:** each change goes to `OfficeState` first (`addPet`, `updatePetProfile`, `removePet`), so the game shows it at once. Then `useEditorActions.commitPets` sends the debounced `saveLayout` and copies the pets into the editor's Reset baseline. No undo entry: the edit happens outside the editor. An editor undo stack from before still holds the old pets.
- **Delete:** inline confirm, then the pet leaves `layout.pets` and `PetCareSystem.forget` drops its needs from `pets-state.json` (saved within a second).
- **+ Pet cat:** places a new pet on the cat template sheet (Gitcat, else the first cat pet) with the name "Kitten" (numbered when taken) and the first breed no pet wears yet. Spawn tile: a free walkable tile closest to pet furniture (`PET|LITTER|SCRATCH|CAT_|YARN|TOY|TUNNEL|BED|HOUSE` in the type), else a random free tile. The spawn is not saved: pets spawn at random on reload, as before.
- **Coats (`office/sprites/petAppearance.ts`):** the cat template sheet is repainted at runtime, frame by frame. Silhouette, frame size and frame count stay the same, so the care poses (`petCareFrames.ts`) derive from the repainted sheet as before. Template colour roles come from the sheet itself: outline, fur, darker shade (Gitcat's markings), reddish nose, lighter inner ear. Body regions (tail, ears, face, eyes, collar, belly, paws, torso) are fixed offsets from the nose, or from the ear tips in the back view. The `Appearance` type, the 13 breed presets, the 18 coat presets and `resolveBreed` are shared with the agent cats. `sweater` paints the torso (Dobby). Repainted sheets are cached by `petType + appearance`.
- **Dogs (decided, feat/layout-no-pets):** the tab lists cats and dogs. **+ Pet dog** places the dog sheet (Claudio, else the first non-cat sheet) named "Puppy" (numbered when taken), with no coat. A dog row shows its manifest name. The dog editor has name, Delete and a big preview, but no coat editor: a coat on a dog entry is ignored.
- **Original look:** the "Original" tile (first in Breeds) removes the coat and shows the template sheet again.
- Server mapping (phase 1): nothing new. The layout and `pets-state.json` messages already exist.

### Phase 1 decisions (feat/orchestrator-core, 1.4.1-cats.11, agent-made)

Code: `server/src/orchestrator/`. UI is phase 2; phase 1 adds only the Settings rows, the task-form "Who" field and the card state.

- **Per-turn process, verified.** Each turn is `claude -p --input-format stream-json --output-format stream-json --verbose --session-id|--resume <id> --append-system-prompt-file --model [--effort] --mcp-config <file> --strict-mcp-config --dangerously-skip-permissions`, cwd = the cat's worktree. One user message, then EOF. Project settings stay on, so cats see the project CLAUDE.md.
- **Cache after `--resume` is hit (measured 2026-10-06, CLI 2.1.290).** Probe (haiku, 2 turns): turn 2 read 29,294 cached tokens and wrote 281. Real e2e run, boss Sonnet: turn 2 after resume read 36,085 and wrote 263; turn 3 read 36,085 and wrote 492. The CLI writes the cache with the 1 h TTL (`ephemeral_1h_input_tokens`). So the long-lived fallback process is NOT built.
- **Cost per turn = delta.** `total_cost_usd` in the `result` event is cumulative over resumes of one session (probe: $0.0328 then $0.0369). The office records the delta per turn.
- **Branch names.** Git cannot hold `task/<id>` and `task/<id>/<cat>` at once (a ref cannot be a file and a folder). Worker branches are `task/<id>-<cat>`. The merged result stays on `task/<id>`.
- **Merges are done by the server, in the parent's name.** When a worker reports, its worktree is committed. Before the parent's next turn, the server merges the branch into the parent's worktree (`--no-ff`) and puts a note in the message. On a conflict the merge stays open and the note tells the parent to resolve and commit. A report never reaches the parent before its branch is merged.
- **Sessions.** One session per cat per task. Turns of one cat are serialized across tasks by the per-cat lock in the turn scheduler. Correction (2026-10-06): a session is no longer bound to its cwd (`--resume` finds a session from any folder since CLI 2.1.223). The session per task stays for the reasons in [context-policy.md](context-policy.md) §3: a clean context per task and one prompt version per task.
- **Characters.** A cat gets a character at its first turn, with its breed as the palette. The character stays idle and linked to the task after the task. The cat's next task replaces it. Superseded in phase 3: every profile is a resident character (see "Wiring decisions").
- **Delivery.** Tools return at once. A message waits in the target's inbox and becomes the user message of its next turn. A message that arrives during a turn waits for the next turn.
- **Guards.** A cat that ends its turn owing a report (nothing to wait for) gets one nudge; after that its last text counts as the report (root: as the final result). A question that the target did not answer with `reply` gets the target's last text as the answer. A task stops with an error after 80 turns (`FLOW_MAX_TURNS`). The root cannot report while a worker still works.
- **Review fixes (medium review, one Opus agent).** A cat may report only when no cat below it still works and no report (or its branch) waits unread; a result or report that a mid-turn report overtakes is dropped, and the cat reads the new report first. A failed turn answers its open questions with the error and drops its report. A merge conflict blocks further merges into that worktree until the cat commits the fix; `commitAll` never commits conflict markers. `endFlow` waits for turns busy with git work, and no process starts after it. Office tools reach only the task's team (the root and the cats below it). Show Guests and the turn cap need the server token, like profile edits.
- **Session lock shared with the cat console.** Every cat turn takes `catTerminal/sessionLocks.ts` (`turn`) before it spawns. When the user holds the session in a terminal (`wheel`), the turn waits and retries every 5 s (`SESSION_LOCK_RETRY_MS`). Office messages show in the cat console as text rows.
- **Team target.** "Team" = the boss. A task can also go to one cat; that cat leads, and it may delegate to its own reports.
- **Model check.** Models and efforts come from `claude --help`: aliases `fable`, `opus`, `sonnet` and efforts `low, medium, high, xhigh, max` on 2.1.290. Help does not list `haiku`, so a Haiku cat uses a full model name (`claude-haiku-4-5-20251001`); the office accepts any `claude-...` full name, as the help text says. The file load checks only the shape, so a CLI upgrade never deletes a cat.
- **One profile model with the Cats menu.** The wire types in `core/asyncapi.yaml` (`CatProfile`, `CatAppearance`, `EngineOptions`) have the same shape as `webview-ui/src/cats/catsApi.ts` (no pets: pets live in `layout.pets`, see the Pets tab section): breed ids are the lower-case breed names, `effort` is required, `parentId: null` marks the one boss, and `isDefault` marks seeded cats. The server keeps the tree rules of `cats/hierarchy.ts` (`server/src/orchestrator/catTree.ts`): exactly one boss, no cycles, reports of a deleted cat move up, a deleted boss hands over to its first report, promote puts the old boss under the new one. A saved cat with `parentId: null` that is not the boss reports to the boss; only `promoteCatToBoss` makes a boss. The office character uses the breed preset as its palette; since phase 3 a custom coat renders in the office too.
- **cats.json.** `{ version: 1, cats: [...] }`, atomic tmp + rename. An unreadable file is copied to `cats.json.bad-<ts>`, then the default team is seeded (boss Opus effort high, three Sonnet workers effort medium). Every change over the WebSocket needs the server token (a cat runs with no permission prompts) and is answered with the full snapshot (`catProfilesLoaded` + `catHierarchy`), or `catProfileRejected` to the requester.
- **Narrator.** `narratorLine`, `narratorSummary`, `narratorSettings` and `setNarratorSettings` are now in `core/asyncapi.yaml`; the casts in `narratorStore.ts` are gone. The office feeds the narrator: tool calls of every cat turn, every office message (sender's character), and the final result.
- **Guests.** With the office on, the standalone server holds back every message about an external session unless Settings → Show Guests is on (default off). Shown guests render translucent. Show Guests and Cats Working at Once (1–12, default 6) persist in config.json like the other settings.
- **MCP transport.** A small hand-written MCP Streamable HTTP endpoint at `/mcp` (JSON responses, no SSE; GET returns 405), one random bearer token per cat per task. No MCP SDK dependency. The token files are removed when the task ends.
- **E2E run (2026-10-06, real CLI, port 4100).** Task "create hello.txt with the word meow and goodbye.txt with the word purr, one file per worker", boss Sonnet + 2 workers Haiku. The boss called brief and two delegates over MCP; both workers reported; both branches were merged into `task/<id>` (two merge commits); result returned to the user. 5 turns (boss 3, workers 1 each), 39 s, total $0.264.

### Wiring decisions (feat/orchestrator-wiring, phase 3, 1.4.1-cats.12, agent-made 2026-10-06)

User bugs of 2026-10-06 and how the wiring answers them.

- **Task form (A).** With cats, "Who" defaults to **Team** (the boss delegates down the hierarchy); a hint under the field says what each choice does. One cat stays selectable; "One cat, plain run (no profile)" is the last option. A cat whose engine CLI is missing is listed but disabled ("Codex CLI not found"); Codex cats run since feat/codex-engine.
- **Resident cats (B, C).** Every profile in cats.json is one office character all the time (`server/src/orchestrator/catResidents.ts`): spawned at server start and on profile create, removed on delete. Between turns it idles (the webview idle activities). A turn points the same character at that turn's session (`beginResidentTurn`: watch its transcript from the end of the file) and marks it active, so it walks to its own seat and works; the turn end idles it again. One character per cat across all tasks and turns. Residents are never persisted (they come back from cats.json); they take ids above the persisted external agents. At a task end every member cat links to the task (clicking it opens the task).
- **New message `catCharacters`** (core/asyncapi.yaml): `{catId, id, name, appearance, working}` per resident, sent on connect after `layoutLoaded` and on every change. The webview sets the name label (`agentName`), the coat (custom appearance -> runtime sprites from `cats/catArt.ts`, the same generator as the Cats menu; a plain breed keeps its char_N sheet) and idle vs working. Since feat/office-polish the idle social poses (talk, angry), the fight cloud paws and the house peek use the custom coat too (`renderSocialAppearance`, from `scripts/cats/social.mjs`).
- **Old-path finished cats survive a restart (C).** `TaskManager.restoreFinishedCats()` gives the 6 newest finished one-cat runs (`TASK_RESTORE_MAX_CATS`) their idle cat back with `finishedTaskId`; tasks a live server owns are skipped. The task takes the new agent id and the restarting server as owner.
- **Cats menu over the server (D).** `cats/serverCatsAdapter.ts`: snapshot from `catProfilesLoaded`, edits apply at once and send the phase-1 messages, a `catProfileRejected` reverts to the last server snapshot and shows inline (Cats menu header, Hierarchy). Engine options come from the server; an engine whose CLI is missing shows as "Codex (Codex CLI not found)" and cannot be picked. The Model field takes an alias from the list or a full name (`claude-...`). Decided: the server snapshot is shown whenever a server office answers, also without the token (edits then come back rejected with the token message); the local adapter is used only when no server office answers (VS Code, Vite dev).
- **One-time import of local cats (D, decided).** Only for a tokened client, only when every server cat is a seeded default, and only when `catavasia.cats.v1` holds cats that differ from the untouched local seed. Parents are saved first, the local boss is promoted, then the server defaults not in the local list are deleted. Cats of an engine the server cannot run are skipped (their reports move up); `haiku` becomes `claude-haiku-4-5-20251001`. A flag `catavasia.cats.migrated.v1` stops a second import from that browser.
- **Scenes (E).** `catOfficeFeed.ts` maps cat ids to resident agent ids: `catMessage` (brief, delegate, ask, reply, report) -> work talks; the brief to `team` plays as the boss speaking to one meeting cat; task / final / nudge lines have no second cat and are skipped. Bubble = the message text (wrapped), tooltip = the full text. `flowStateChanged` now carries `rootCatId` and `catIds` (the task's team) for the briefing meeting. The server sends `interrupted` on shutdown; the webview treats it like `error`, and a lost connection ends every open briefing.
- **Queue (F).** Cats whose turn waits for a slot (queued, not running) stand in a line from the coffee spot outward (BFS over walkable tiles), with an "in queue #n" marker. The line tiles go into the reservations like meeting chairs. A talk or meeting takes a cat out of the line; it returns after.
- **Cat console for profile cats (G).** `server/src/catTerminal/officeCatSource.ts` (falls through to the task-board source for other ids). The chat shows the cat's turns across its tasks (in memory, 400 rows, empty after a restart). Send: during a live task the text waits in the cat's inbox (`[Message from the user]`) and its next turn reads it; a cat with no live task gets a new one-cat task in the folder of its newest task. The wheel: only between turns of a live task (no running or queued turn), with the shared `wheel` session lock; turns wait while it is held, and the task end waits too (its worktrees stay until the user gives the wheel back).
- **Guard.** A delegate / ask to a cat whose engine has no adapter is refused with a tool error (before, the node stayed "working" forever).
- **Flaky test (H).** `catLife.test.ts` seeds the contest roll (no fight) and the idle picks: an idle cat could claim the bed a moment after the forced claim and win a contest fight (about 5 % of runs).
- **E2E (2026-10-06, real CLI, port 5600, real HOME, files restored byte for byte).** Boss Sonnet + two Haiku workers (one with a custom calico coat). Two team tasks through the UI ("create a.txt with 'meow' and b.txt with 'purr', one file per worker", then c.txt / d.txt): residents idle before, briefing meeting with "Briefing the team", delegate and report bubbles, workers typing at their seats, back to idle after, results on the cards, the worker's console shows its turns of both tasks. Task 1: 5 turns, $0.276; task 2: 5 turns, 42 s, $0.246; total $0.52.
- **Office polish (feat/office-polish, agent-made 2026-10-06).** The task card and detail "Cat" row show the profile name, or "Team · <boss>" for a team task (breed name only for a plain run). A work-talk sender walks to a reachable free tile next to the receiver (left / right first), else up to `SCENE_TALK_FALLBACK_RADIUS` (2) tiles away; a seated receiver that gets up during the walk-up is stopped and the sender re-plans. With no reachable tile, or after the approach timeout, the sender stays put and the line shows over both cats. An idle cat's narrator line gives way 5 s after it arrived (`NARRATOR_IDLE_AFTER_MS`) to an idle line from its activity ("sipping coffee", "taking a catnap", "on a stroll", "playing", ...). A waiting line ("meowing for you") stays.
- **Known limits.** While the 5600 test server ran, it owned `server.json`, so the hook script reported to it (restored afterwards).

### Task state machine and context policy (feat/task-state-machine, 1.4.1-cats.13, agent-made 2026-10-06)

Specs: [task-state-machine.md](task-state-machine.md), [context-policy.md](context-policy.md). Code: `server/src/orchestrator/machine/` (pure reducer + interpreter + event log), `promptFile.ts`, `promptRepo.ts`.

- **Built as specified:** T1-T15, A1-A18, R1-R9, invariants checked after every event in tests (a violation in production ends the task with an error). Event log + snapshot in `~/.pixel-agents/flows/<taskId>/`, logs older than 30 days are deleted at start. Restart -> `interrupted` until the user presses Resume; Cancel on a running or interrupted task. Turn timeout 30 min, one retry after 10 s for an infrastructure failure, `delegate` with `rework: true`. The Cat CEO review region is a seam: `catCeo` is off, so a finished task gets `review_skipped`.

## Resource budget (RAM) — plan to reach the goal cheaper

Measured 2026-10-05 on this Mac: one-shot `claude -p --model haiku` = ~305 MB peak RSS, 15.6 s wall time incl. the model reply. The research measured 300–850 MB per long-lived session.

1. DECIDED (user, 2026-10-05): process per turn, not per cat. Measure the cache-hit rate after `--resume` first. If the cache is lost, fall back to a long-lived process that sleeps after 1 h idle. The prompt cache lives on Anthropic's side, not in the local process. A cat process can exit right after its turn and `--resume` on the next message without losing the cache while the TTL holds. RAM is used only by cats that are working right now. The "sleep after 1 h" rule then only affects the game look, not RAM.
   Cost: a few seconds of startup per turn. Verify the cache-hit rate after a resume in the usage fields before relying on it.
2. Concurrency cap (user, 2026-10-05): at most N cats run a turn at the same time. Default 6. Settings offer 1 to 6 or more. Other cats queue. The game shows them waiting in line, for example at the coffee machine.
3. DECIDED (user, 2026-10-05), replaces the always-on narrator: narrator without a resident process: templates in the server for routine events (read file, run tests, edit). Haiku only for conversation summaries and the task brief, in batched one-shot calls. A long-lived narrator process is not needed.
4. Leaf workers as subagents (optional): a worker that never delegates can run as a subagent inside its parent's process. That uses less RAM, but the user cannot open its own terminal. Off by default.
   Expected peak: about N × 300–850 MB while N cats work, near 0 when the office is idle.

### Narrator decisions (feat/narrator, phase 2c, agent-made 2026-10-06)

## Office scenes (feat/office-scenes, phase 2d, agent-made decisions)

- Client interface: `webview-ui/src/orchestratorEvents.ts`, one bus `orchestratorEvents`. - Work talk: the sender walks next to the receiver. A receiver seated at its desk stays seated. Any other receiver stops and turns to the sender. The sender's bubble shows 4 s, then it waits up to 6 s for a reply (any message from the receiver to the sender). Then the sender walks back to its desk and both cats return to the FSM. A queued answer of the receiver joins the talk when it starts. A later reply becomes its own talk.
- Queue: a cat is in one talk at a time. Pending talks start in arrival order per cat; a talk waits while either cat is busy or has an older pending talk.
- Bubble: summary if present, else the text; word-wrapped to 2 lines of 20 chars, "…" when cut. DOM overlay (`components/SceneBubbleOverlay.tsx`) in the pixel font, kind badge in the corner. Two bubbles of a pair grow away from each other. Hover on a bubble or on either cat shows the tooltip with the full summaries of the pair.
- Meeting room: detected at runtime, no layout change (adding an Area to the default layout would only reach new installs without a revision bump). An Area whose label contains "meeting" wins; otherwise the biggest desk-category table with 2 or more chairs next to it. In the default office that is the main room work table (4 chairs, boss head tile above it).
- Meeting: participants sit on the room's chairs (own seat if it is there, else a chair no agent owns). Chairs taken for the meeting go into the reconcile as `seat` reservations, so they outrank a napping cat. Cats without a chair stand next to the table. The boss stands at the head and shows a bubble ("Briefing the team", replaced by the boss's latest `brief` message). Messages between two meeting cats play in place, one talk per cat at a time; messages for a meeting cat from outside wait. When the task leaves `briefing`, everyone walks back to its desk. A new briefing of another task ends the current meeting.
- Known limits: fake hook sessions stay pending (external sessions need confirmation), so the screenshots added agents with the test hooks. A user command (seat / walk) on a cat in a scene is overridden by the scene. A new agent may get a free chair that a meeting holds. A meeting ends only on a non-briefing `flowStateChanged` of the same task (or a new briefing): phase 1 must always send it, also on abort.

## Self-update (feat/self-update, 1.4.1-cats.20, agent-made 2026-10-06)

User request: "the game should check for new versions on the main branch of my git by itself, offer to update, and update itself if the user approves."

- Check: the server reads `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Poxagronka/catavasia/main/package.json` at start and every 6 h while Settings "Check for updates automatically" is on (default on, setting `autoUpdateCheck`). Settings also has "Check for updates" and a status line ("Up to date", "X is available", "Last check failed: …"). A failed check shows nowhere else.
- Version order: own 25-line semver comparator (`server/src/update/version.ts`), not the `semver` package: it is a root devDependency only, and adding it to the server workspace would change the lockfile for one function. Prerelease parts compare numerically (`cats.10` > `cats.9`).
- "What's new": esbuild stamps the build's git commit (`CATAVASIA_COMMIT`). The check calls the GitHub compare API `<commit>...main` once per new version, unauthenticated, and lists non-merge commit titles, newest first, max 50. No commit or an API failure (rate limit 60/h) → a link to the GitHub compare (or commits) page.
- Offer: a top-center panel "New version X is available — Update / Later / What's new". "Later" is kept in server memory per version, so it lasts until a newer version or the next server start, and survives a page reload. A manual "Check for updates" takes "Later" back.
- Transport: plain HTTP routes `/api/update*` polled by the webview (5 min idle, 1 s during an install), not new WS messages: no AsyncAPI change, same pattern as `/api/tasks`. All update routes need the token, also the GET: its log tail carries local paths.
- Install (only on "Update"): `git clone --depth 1 --branch main` into a fresh `~/.pixel-agents/update/work-*`, then `npm ci --include=dev`, `npm run package` (the production build `prepack` runs), `npm pack --ignore-scripts` (no second build), `npm install -g ./<tgz>` over the existing install (no uninstall: npm replaces the package), `npm root -g` to find the new `dist/cli.js`. Commands run through `spawn` with `shell: false` (same safety as execFile, plus streamed lines). The work dir is always removed. The log stays in `~/.pixel-agents/update/update-<time>.log`. A failure leaves the running server as it is and shows the step, the error and the log path.
- Busy: Update is refused while any task is `running` or any cat turn runs or waits; the panel shows the reason with the task titles. A task started during the build does not get killed: after the install the runner waits (re-checks every 5 s) until the office is idle, then restarts. No queue before the build starts (the user presses again).
- Restart: the old server spawns `node <new cli.js> --port <same> --host <same>` detached, then after 1.5 s runs the normal SIGTERM shutdown, so tasks become `interrupted` → Resume, as on any restart. The new process waits until the old pid has exited before it binds, and exits with an error after 20 s instead of reusing the old server's registry entry.
- Token: the old token goes to the new process in the `CATAVASIA_RESTART_TOKEN` env var (not argv: argv is visible to all users in `ps`). The new process deletes it from its env at once (so claude/git/npm children never inherit it) and passes it to `PixelAgentsServer.start({ token })`, which writes it into the usual `servers/*.json` record. The child's stdout is discarded (the CLI prints the tokened URL there); stderr goes to `~/.pixel-agents/update/restart.log`.
- Reload: the tab shows "Restarting…", polls `/api/health` (now with `version`) until another pid or version answers, then reloads. After 2 min it shows a manual start hint and the log path.
- Test knob: `CATAVASIA_UPDATE_BRANCH` follows another branch of the SAME fixed repo (used for the sandbox run, since main had no updater yet). `CATAVASIA_COMMIT` at build time overrides the stamped commit.
- Known limits: macOS/Linux only (`npm`/`git` without `.cmd`); a new version that fails to start leaves no server running (start it by hand, see `restart.log`); the server started by `pa` in a terminal moves to a detached process after an update, so its logs no longer show in that terminal; raw.githubusercontent.com caches up to 5 min, so a fresh merge shows up a little later.

## Stable server token (fix/stable-token, agent-made 2026-10-06)

User symptom: after a server restart, open tabs showed "Prompt history needs the server token" and every edit failed. A reload of a tab opened without `?token=` also lost edit rights. Cause: every start made a new random token, and the page read the token only from the URL.

- **One token across restarts** (`server/src/authToken.ts`): the standalone CLI keeps its token in `~/.pixel-agents/auth-token`. The file is created with mode 0600 (tmp file + rename, so it is never readable by others). A file that is not a regular file, is not owned by the user, has group/other mode bits, or holds a token shorter than 16 characters is replaced with a new token, not repaired: a loose file may have leaked. If the file cannot be written, the server still starts with a token for this run only. The self-update path (`CATAVASIA_RESTART_TOKEN`) still works and now hands over the same token.
- **Standalone only**: the VS Code embedded server keeps a fresh in-process token per start. It has no URL token, so it never had the symptom.

## Install UX: Desktop launcher and browser start (feat/install-ux, 1.4.1-cats.40, agent-made 2026-10-06)

User requirement: build the convenient start of the owner's private `pa` helper into the install itself. Install → Desktop launcher → double-click → the office opens at `http://127.0.0.1:3100`.

- **Default port 3100** (`server/src/launch/portChoice.ts`). Before the asset load, the CLI reads this HOME's registry (`~/.pixel-agents/servers/*.json`, live PID) and probes the port with a TCP connect. Our standalone server listens there → open its token URL, exit 0. Port free → start on it. Another program holds it → open our server on another port when one runs, else start on an OS-assigned port with a one-line note. An explicit `--port N` never falls back: a busy port keeps the "Port N is busy" error. The older in-process "Reusing existing standalone server" path in `server.ts` stays as the second guard.
- **Opening the browser** (`launch/openBrowser.ts`): macOS `open`, Linux `xdg-open`, Windows `cmd /c start ""` (verbatim arguments). A missing opener prints the URL, never throws. It opens after the server listens, and also when `--port N` is given (the `pa` flow and the real check use that).
- **No tab on a self-update restart**: the replacement process gets the token in `CATAVASIA_RESTART_TOKEN`. An inherited token turns the open off. `--no-open` and `CATAVASIA_NO_OPEN=1` also turn it off. The server vitest config sets `CATAVASIA_NO_OPEN=1`, e2e and the npm smoke pass `--no-open`, so no test opens a real browser.
- **`CATAVASIA_PORT`** overrides the default port. It exists so the launcher itself can be tested on a free port without touching a live 3100.
- **Launcher** (`launch/shortcut.ts`, `catavasia shortcut [--remove]`): macOS `~/Desktop/catavasia.command`, Linux `~/.local/share/applications/catavasia.desktop` plus a Desktop copy when `~/Desktop` exists (`Terminal=true`, `Icon=` the shipped `icon.png`), Windows `Desktop\catavasia.cmd`. It runs the absolute global bin (`<prefix>/bin/catavasia`, Windows `<prefix>\catavasia.cmd`), or `node <checkout>/dist/cli.js` outside a global install. Every file carries the marker `catavasia-launcher`. No Desktop folder on macOS or Windows → no file, a hint to run `catavasia`. The macOS custom icon is skipped: it needs Finder or AppKit calls, not cheap and not safe from a postinstall.
- **postinstall** (`launch/postinstall.ts` → `dist/postinstall.js`, in `files`): runs only when `npm_config_global=true` or `npm_config_location=global`. The package.json script is `node -e "try{require('./dist/postinstall.js').runPostinstall()}catch{}"`, so `npm ci` in the repo (no `dist/` yet, not global) does nothing and never fails. It prints one line, but npm 7+ hides install-script output, so `install.sh` prints how to start. `--ignore-scripts` skips it: docs say run `catavasia shortcut`. The in-game update runs `npm install -g`, so each update writes the launcher again (same content).
- **Uninstall**: npm 7+ runs no uninstall scripts, so there is no `preuninstall`. The documented step `node "$(npm root -g)/catavasia/dist/uninstall.js"` (already the hook-removal step) now also removes the launcher files, by exact path and marker only. `catavasia shortcut --remove` removes only the launcher. Side effect: the VS Code extension uninstall runs the same file, so it also removes a CLI launcher of the same user.
- **Tab title**: `webview-ui/index.html` `<title>` is `catavasia`.

## Automatic npm publish (ci/npm-auto-publish, 1.4.1-cats.41, agent-made 2026-10-06)

User requirement: publish every new version to npm through a GitHub workflow. Each merge to `main` bumps the version +1.

- **New workflow `publish-npm.yml`**, not `scripts/publish-npm-package.mjs`. The script is bound to GitHub Release tags (`refs/tags/v<version>`) and fails on a re-run with a different tarball integrity. The fork releases on push to `main`, so the workflow skips a version that `npm view` already finds and publishes the verified tarball directly.
- **Triggers**: push to `main` and `workflow_dispatch`. A `check` job reads `package.json` and runs `npm view catavasia@<version>`. Found → stop. E404 → publish. Any other npm error → fail, never a blind publish.
- **Gate in the publish job**: lint, check-types, format:check, `verify:npm-package` (production build + install smoke test of the tarball), package contract, webview tests, server tests with `--testTimeout=60000`. knip and e2e stay in `ci.yml` only.
- **Auth**: npm trusted publishing (OIDC). The job has `id-token: write` and upgrades npm to `^11.15.0` (trusted publishing needs >=11.5.1, staged publishing >=11.15.0). Node comes from `.nvmrc` (22, latest patch >=22.14.0). No npm token is stored anywhere.
- **Direct `npm publish --tag latest`**, not `npm stage publish`: a staged version waits for the owner's manual 2FA approval with the hardware key, which defeats automation. The owner must allow `npm publish` in the trusted publisher's Allowed actions.
- **Concurrency** group `publish-npm`, no cancel: two merges publish one after the other. The second run sees its own new version.
- **Upstream-only jobs**: both jobs in `publish-extension.yml` get `if: github.repository == 'pixel-agents-hq/pixel-agents'`. A guard, not a deletion, keeps upstream merges simple. `update-badges.yml` already has this guard. `ci.yml` stays: its only secrets (Vercel) sit behind a precheck that skips the deploy when they are missing.
- **Owner steps** (agent cannot do them): enable Actions on the fork, configure the npm trusted publisher. See CONTRIBUTING.md, "Maintainer release checklist".

## Pet interactions (feat/pet-interactions, agent-made 2026-10-07)

User requirement: the pet cats get custom animations for every object and toy the agent cats use. Audit and matrix: `docs/catavasia/pet-interactions.md`.

- **Poses are derived, not drawn.** New pet poses (rear up, scratch, bat, reach, pin, curl, box peek, climb, perch) come from the pet's own sheet in `sprites/petPlayFrames.ts`, like the care poses (`petCareFrames.ts`). Reason: pet sheets have a fixed 96x96 layout (`core/src/assets/constants.ts`), coats are repainted at runtime, and custom pets come from external asset folders. A derived pose works for every coat and every custom pet with no art and no sheet format change. No `scripts/generate-*-sprites.mjs` change was needed.
- **Steps live in `engine/petPlayAnims.ts`**, one table per toy, the pet's version of `toyAnims.ts`. The care runtime only counts time (`careAnim.t`/`dur`); the renderer, the effects and the toy motion read the step at that time. Each table has a `base` offset: the spots are the agent cats' spots, and a pet's side pose is wider and stands on the tile centre line.
- **Every playroom activity is a pet claim**: scratch, yarn, mouse, teaser, box, cat tree, tunnel. Beds and the cat bed play `curl`; houses and sofas keep the loaf (`sleep`) because a house shows only the peek and a sofa seat fits the front loaf. The tunnel claim lasts exactly its animation (`fixed`), so a pet never vanishes mid-run.
- **Joint play**: a pet's turn at yarn, mouse or teaser now plays that toy's pose and moves the toy. Box, cat tree and tunnel claims put pets into the playroom group, so they now talk to and chase agent cats there.
- **Out of scope by design**: coffee (pets never drink coffee), the bookshelf (a work activity), desks and chairs (agent work). The bowl and litter boxes stay in the care loop. The dog pet (Claudio) has no care loop and no claims, as before.
- **Orientation**: pets use the agent spot sets, which `itemFrame` turns. The guard test (`furnitureRotation.test.ts`) now claims every pet activity at every item × view and draws every step. A side pose flips for a LEFT facing; a front pose flips with a mirrored item.
- **Tooling**: `npx tsx scripts/preview-pets.ts [--only <activity>]` writes a GIF, a strip and four large frames per item view × pet activity. The dev hook `__pixelAgentsTestHooks.forcePetActivity(petId, activityId, spotKey?)` (e2e only) sends a pet to an activity, for real-app screenshots.
- **Skill tail**: this run used the add-furniture skill as a reference (spots, guard, previews), not as a furniture run, so its JUDGE → IMPROVE → LEDGER tail was not run. The skill gained one line on pet poses.
- **Known limits**: the curled pet on a 1x1 bed is wider than the bed. At the top end of a vertical tunnel the waiting pet stands a few px above the opening.
