# catavasia roadmap (user requirements, 2026-10-05)

Design research and evidence: [orchestration-spec.md](orchestration-spec.md).

## Integration step (after feat/idle-activities, feat/cat-playroom, feat/cat-social, feat/pet-care land)

1. Wire the social layer (talk, joint play, fights) into the idle-activity registry: coffee spot, playroom toys.
2. Pets join the idle pool: playroom toys, sofa sleep, fights. Pets never drink coffee.
3. Furniture: pet beds (round cushion, basket, radiator hammock, donut blanket) and cat houses (cardboard house with window, felt igloo cave, wooden house with roof, two-storey condo). Sleeping inside shows only ears or tail. Pets get a new need "sleep/energy" and walk to the nearest free bed/house. Agent cats may sleep there too, as an idle variant.
4. Spot reservation (requirement): a cat (agent or pet) reserves a spot (seat, bed, house, toy, bowl) when it STARTS walking to it. No other cat may target a reserved or occupied spot.
5. Contention (requirement): when two cats claim the same spot at the same moment (idle choice or need), there is a probability of a fight, using the social-layer fight scene. The loser picks another spot.

### Integration decisions (feat/integration, 1.4.1-cats.9, agent-made while the user slept)

- One service, `engine/spotReservations.ts`, keyed by tile. `engine/spotClaims.ts` wraps it for the office; `engine/catLife.ts` wires it to idle activities, pet care (bowls, litter box, provider claims), joint play and seat assignment.
- A cat reserves at walk start. A per-frame reconcile releases every key its holder no longer wants (arrived then left, interrupted, scene over), drops keys of despawned holders, and syncs assigned seats in as `seat` reservations.
- A seat outranks a spot: when the user seats an agent where a cat naps, the cat gives way. A new agent never gets a sofa seat a cat holds.
- Contest = a claim on a spot another cat reserved less than `SPOT_CONTEST_WINDOW_SEC` (1.5 s) ago and has not reached. The roll is `SPOT_CONTEST_FIGHT_CHANCE` (0.35) for a fight, winner 50/50; otherwise the second cat re-picks. No fight beyond `SPOT_CONTEST_FIGHT_RADIUS_TILES` (8) or inside the pair's social cooldown: the second cat re-picks.
- The fight winner holds the spot through the scene and walks back to it when the scene ends; the loser picks something else.
- Pets join social scenes through stand-in actor characters (`engine/petActors.ts`, ids from `PET_ACTOR_ID_BASE`). Pets have no talk/angry art: they show the pictogram bubbles, anger marks and the dust cloud over their own sprite. Only cat pets take part.
- Pets idle-play with yarn, mouse, teaser and scratching post (hop pose); they nap on beds, in houses, on the cat bed, on sofa seats or the floor by a sofa. No box, cat tree or tunnel for pets (agent-only poses). Pets never get a coffee claim.
- Energy: 6th need, decays 8/office-hour while awake, refills 2.5/real-second asleep, wakes early when full. Under 35 a pet seeks the nearest free bed/house, then sofa, then floor. Energy raises no request bubble. Old `pets-state.json` loads with fresh energy (no version bump).
- Activity social: every 2 s, chance 0.35, the closest pair at coffee or in the playroom talks in place; in the playroom 60 % of those become joint play: turns at a yarn ball, a mouse or a teaser, a chase when one of them is at the tunnel.
- Furniture: new "Cat beds" catalog tab, 4 beds and 4 houses generated by `scripts/generate-bed-sprites.mjs` (MIT). Inside a house only ears (cardboard, condo) or a tail (igloo, wooden) show, recolored with the cat's fur; near-black cats get a light rim.
- Known limits: joint play on a one-sided toy (teaser) puts the second cat behind the first; pet idle frames face the viewer, so a waiting pet in joint play looks at the camera; reservations cover activity spots, not tiles where a cat merely stands.

## Orchestration (spec: orchestration-spec.md). User decisions

- Engine: own orchestrator. Each cat is a long-lived `claude -p` stream-json session owned by the catavasia server. An HTTP MCP server in Fastify gives the tools brief, delegate, report, ask, reply, list_team. The server enforces the hierarchy.
- Worktrees: one worktree per worker cat. The boss merges the workers' branches into the task branch.
- Models: boss Opus, workers Sonnet, narrator Haiku 4.5. Each can be changed per cat.
- Lifecycle: sessions run on demand, sleep after 1 hour of idle, and resume. Rationale (user): by then the prompt cache has expired anyway, so a live process gives no saving. Tie the threshold to the session's real cache TTL: read it from the stream-json usage fields (ephemeral 5m vs 1h cache creation) and verify. Default 1 h as a constant with --resume. The cat stays visible in the game.
- Defaults I chose: ask goes up/down the tree plus siblings. The narrator batches every 10 s. Permissions are skipped, as for tasks. Reuse upstream PR #347 (PTY + xterm.js) in the fork.
- Terminal: a chat console by default. "Take the wheel" opens a real `claude --resume` PTY when the cat is idle. A per-cat lock prevents two processes on one session.
- Narrator: superseded, see the RAM plan item 3 (templates + batched one-shot Haiku calls). Short English text. The server validates its output against a schema.
- Office shows ONLY own cats from the Cats menu. External claude sessions appear as guests, behind a Settings toggle that is OFF by default.
- One session = one cat. Subagents are not shown (fix/one-cat-per-session in progress).
- The Cats menu: Agents tab (look, name, prompt, role, model, effort: passed as `--model` / `--effort`; list the values the installed `claude --help` accepts, do not hardcode from memory), Hierarchy tab (tree editor), Pets tab (create, name, colour/pattern from the shared editor with more colours).
- A task flows boss → briefing meeting (all cats gather in one room) → delegation down → work → reports up → result to the user. Cats walk to each other for every conversation. Speech bubbles appear, and hover shows the narrator summary.

### Cat terminal decisions (feat/cat-terminal, phase 2b, agent-made)

- Click a cat, then `>_` on its selected label: a docked panel opens (Chat tab default, Terminal tab). The task-modal click on a finished task cat stays unchanged.
- Client contract `webview-ui/src/catTerminal/catSessionApi.ts`: `subscribe(catId, onEvent, onGone?)`, `send(catId, text)`, `canTakeWheel(catId)`, `takeWheel(catId, size, handlers)`, `releaseWheel(catId)`. Wire frames live in `core/src/catSession.ts`.
- Server endpoints (`server/src/catTerminal/`): WS `/api/cat-sessions/:catId/events` (snapshot, then `entries` and `status` frames; same-origin only), POST `/api/cat-sessions/:catId/messages` `{text}` (token), WS `/api/cat-sessions/:catId/terminal?token=&cols=&rows=` (token + same-origin).
- Phase 1 plugs in by implementing `CatSessionSource` (`snapshot`, `subscribe`, `send`, `beginWheel`, `endWheel`) and passing it where `httpServer.ts` builds `TaskBoardCatSource`. Today `catId` is the office agent id of a task-board cat. Phase 1 may use its own cat ids.
- Lock: `catTerminal/sessionLocks.ts` holds one owner (`turn` or `wheel`) per session id. Phase 1 MUST take this same lock before it spawns a turn. A message or the wheel during a running turn gets HTTP 409 / WS close 4409.
- A message to a task cat is one more headless turn: `claude --resume <id> -p` in the task worktree (checked out again from `task/<id>`), committed again when it ends. No mid-turn injection yet: the `priority: "now"` stream-json input needs phase 1's long-lived stdin. Streaming = rows arrive live per stream-json record, no `--include-partial-messages` token deltas.
- The wheel: `claude --resume <id>` (interactive, normal permission prompts) in a PTY with cwd = the session's worktree. The PTY lives as long as its socket: closing the tab or "Give it back" kills it, commits the worktree and frees the lock. No headless-xterm replay on reload (upstream PR #347 has it; not needed for a one-socket drawer).
- node-pty: `@lydell/node-pty` (optional dependency, prebuilt, verified to spawn on this Mac, Node 24). Loader ported from PR #347 `ptyModule.ts`. Without a working module the Terminal tab shows the reason and the chat still works.
- Known limits: StrictMode double-mount in `vite dev` takes the wheel twice (the second gets 409). Cats without a task session show "no session" in the panel.

- The agent UI talks only to `webview-ui/src/cats/catsApi.ts` (`CatsApi`). Today `localCatsAdapter.ts` implements it in memory and mirrors it to `localStorage` key `catavasia.cats.v1`. Phase 1 had pushed no `core` types when this landed, so the names are ours.

Colours are `#rrggbb`. Breed ids are the lower-case breed names (`marmalade` … `bear`).

The static lists in the local adapter are marked TODO-phase1, and the Codex values are unverified.
- Hierarchy rule: exactly one boss (`parentId === null`). The server must enforce the same rules as `cats/hierarchy.ts`: no cycles, a deleted cat's reports move up, a deleted boss hands over to its first report, "promote to boss" puts the old boss under the new one.
- Sprites: the webview reuses `scripts/cats/breeds.mjs` + `poses.mjs` directly (typed by `.d.mts` files), so custom looks render at runtime with no server round trip. `cats/catArt.ts` turns an `Appearance` into a breed object. 13 breed presets plus 18 coat presets for both agents and pets.
- Delete uses an inline confirm button: VS Code webviews block `window.confirm`.

### Phase 1 decisions (feat/orchestrator-core, 1.4.1-cats.11, agent-made)

Code: `server/src/orchestrator/`. UI is phase 2; phase 1 adds only the Settings rows, the task-form "Who" field and the card state.

- **Per-turn process, verified.** Each turn is `claude -p --input-format stream-json --output-format stream-json --verbose --session-id|--resume <id> --append-system-prompt-file --model [--effort] --mcp-config <file> --strict-mcp-config --dangerously-skip-permissions`, cwd = the cat's worktree. One user message, then EOF. Project settings stay on, so cats see the project CLAUDE.md.
- **Cache after `--resume` is hit (measured 2026-10-06, CLI 2.1.290).** Probe (haiku, 2 turns): turn 2 read 29,294 cached tokens and wrote 281. Real e2e run, boss Sonnet: turn 2 after resume read 36,085 and wrote 263; turn 3 read 36,085 and wrote 492. The CLI writes the cache with the 1 h TTL (`ephemeral_1h_input_tokens`). So the long-lived fallback process is NOT built.
- **Cost per turn = delta.** `total_cost_usd` in the `result` event is cumulative over resumes of one session (probe: $0.0328 then $0.0369). The office records the delta per turn.
- **Branch names.** Git cannot hold `task/<id>` and `task/<id>/<cat>` at once (a ref cannot be a file and a folder). Worker branches are `task/<id>-<cat>`. The merged result stays on `task/<id>`.
- **Merges are done by the server, in the parent's name.** When a worker reports, its worktree is committed. Before the parent's next turn, the server merges the branch into the parent's worktree (`--no-ff`) and puts a note in the message. On a conflict the merge stays open and the note tells the parent to resolve and commit. A report never reaches the parent before its branch is merged.
- **Sessions.** One session per cat per task (a task has its own worktrees, and a session is bound to its cwd). Turns of one cat are serialized across tasks by the per-cat lock in the turn scheduler.
- **Characters.** A cat gets a character at its first turn, with its breed as the palette. The character stays idle and linked to the task after the task. The cat's next task replaces it. Superseded in phase 3: every profile is a resident character (see "Wiring decisions").
- **Delivery.** Tools return at once. A message waits in the target's inbox and becomes the user message of its next turn. A message that arrives during a turn waits for the next turn.
- **Guards.** A cat that ends its turn owing a report (nothing to wait for) gets one nudge; after that its last text counts as the report (root: as the final result). A question that the target did not answer with `reply` gets the target's last text as the answer. A task stops with an error after 80 turns (`FLOW_MAX_TURNS`). The root cannot report while a worker still works.
- **Review fixes (medium review, one Opus agent).** A cat may report only when no cat below it still works and no report (or its branch) waits unread; a result or report that a mid-turn report overtakes is dropped, and the cat reads the new report first. A failed turn answers its open questions with the error and drops its report. A merge conflict blocks further merges into that worktree until the cat commits the fix; `commitAll` never commits conflict markers. `endFlow` waits for turns busy with git work, and no process starts after it. Office tools reach only the task's team (the root and the cats below it). Show Guests and the turn cap need the server token, like profile edits.
- **Session lock shared with the cat console.** Every cat turn takes `catTerminal/sessionLocks.ts` (`turn`) before it spawns. When the user holds the session in a terminal (`wheel`), the turn waits and retries every 5 s (`SESSION_LOCK_RETRY_MS`). Office messages show in the cat console as text rows.
- **Team target.** "Team" = the boss. A task can also go to one cat; that cat leads, and it may delegate to its own reports.
- **Model check.** Models and efforts come from `claude --help`: aliases `fable`, `opus`, `sonnet` and efforts `low, medium, high, xhigh, max` on 2.1.290. Help does not list `haiku`, so a Haiku cat uses a full model name (`claude-haiku-4-5-20251001`); the office accepts any `claude-...` full name, as the help text says. The file load checks only the shape, so a CLI upgrade never deletes a cat.
- **One profile model with the Cats menu.** The wire types in `core/asyncapi.yaml` (`CatProfile`, `CatAppearance`, `EngineOptions`) have the same shape as `webview-ui/src/cats/catsApi.ts` (no pets: pets live in `layout.pets`, see the Pets tab section): breed ids are the lower-case breed names, `effort` is required, `parentId: null` marks the one boss, and `isDefault` marks seeded cats. The server keeps the tree rules of `cats/hierarchy.ts` (`server/src/orchestrator/catTree.ts`): exactly one boss, no cycles, reports of a deleted cat move up, a deleted boss hands over to its first report, promote puts the old boss under the new one. A saved cat with `parentId: null` that is not the boss reports to the boss; only `promoteCatToBoss` makes a boss. The office character uses the breed preset as its palette; since phase 3 a custom coat renders in the office too.
- **cats.json.** `{ version: 1, cats: [...] }`, atomic tmp + rename. An unreadable file is copied to `cats.json.bad-<ts>`, then the default team is seeded (boss Opus effort high, three Sonnet workers effort medium). Every change over the WebSocket needs the server token (a cat runs with no permission prompts) and is answered with the full snapshot (`catProfilesLoaded` + `catHierarchy`), or `catProfileRejected` to the requester.
- **Narrator.** `narratorLine`, `narratorSummary`, `narratorSettings` and `setNarratorSettings` are now in `core/asyncapi.yaml`; the casts in `narratorStore.ts` are gone. The office feeds the narrator: tool calls of every cat turn, every office message (sender's character), and the final result.
- **Guests.** With the office on, the standalone server holds back every message about an external session unless Settings → Show Guests is on (default off). Shown guests render translucent. Show Guests and Cats Working at Once (1–12, default 6) persist in config.json like the other settings.
- **MCP transport.** A small hand-written MCP Streamable HTTP endpoint at `/mcp` (JSON responses, no SSE; GET returns 405), one random bearer token per cat per task. No MCP SDK dependency. The token files are removed when the task ends.
- **E2E run (2026-10-06, real CLI, port 4100).** Task "create hello.txt with the word meow and goodbye.txt with the word purr, one file per worker", boss Sonnet + 2 workers Haiku. The boss called brief and two delegates over MCP; both workers reported; both branches were merged into `task/<id>` (two merge commits); result returned to the user. 5 turns (boss 3, workers 1 each), 39 s, total $0.264.

### Wiring decisions (feat/orchestrator-wiring, phase 3, 1.4.1-cats.12, agent-made 2026-10-06)

User bugs of 2026-10-06 and how the wiring answers them.

- **Task form (A).** With cats, "Who" defaults to **Team** (the boss delegates down the hierarchy); a hint under the field says what each choice does. One cat stays selectable; "One cat, plain run (no profile)" is the last option. A Codex cat is listed but disabled ("codex adapter not ready").
- **Resident cats (B, C).** Every profile in cats.json is one office character all the time (`server/src/orchestrator/catResidents.ts`): spawned at server start and on profile create, removed on delete. Between turns it idles (the webview idle activities). A turn points the same character at that turn's session (`beginResidentTurn`: watch its transcript from the end of the file) and marks it active, so it walks to its own seat and works; the turn end idles it again. One character per cat across all tasks and turns. Residents are never persisted (they come back from cats.json); they take ids above the persisted external agents. At a task end every member cat links to the task (clicking it opens the task).
- **New message `catCharacters`** (core/asyncapi.yaml): `{catId, id, name, appearance, working}` per resident, sent on connect after `layoutLoaded` and on every change. The webview sets the name label (`agentName`), the coat (custom appearance -> runtime sprites from `cats/catArt.ts`, the same generator as the Cats menu; a plain breed keeps its char_N sheet) and idle vs working. Social poses and the house peek still use the breed palette.
- **Old-path finished cats survive a restart (C).** `TaskManager.restoreFinishedCats()` gives the 6 newest finished one-cat runs (`TASK_RESTORE_MAX_CATS`) their idle cat back with `finishedTaskId`; tasks a live server owns are skipped. The task takes the new agent id and the restarting server as owner.
- **Cats menu over the server (D).** `cats/serverCatsAdapter.ts`: snapshot from `catProfilesLoaded`, edits apply at once and send the phase-1 messages, a `catProfileRejected` reverts to the last server snapshot and shows inline (Cats menu header, Hierarchy). Engine options come from the server; Codex stays listed as "(adapter not ready)" and cannot be picked. The Model field takes an alias from the list or a full name (`claude-...`). Decided: the server snapshot is shown whenever a server office answers, also without the token (edits then come back rejected with the token message); the local adapter is used only when no server office answers (VS Code, Vite dev).
- **One-time import of local cats (D, decided).** Only for a tokened client, only when every server cat is a seeded default, and only when `catavasia.cats.v1` holds cats that differ from the untouched local seed. Parents are saved first, the local boss is promoted, then the server defaults not in the local list are deleted. Codex cats are skipped (their reports move up); `haiku` becomes `claude-haiku-4-5-20251001`. A flag `catavasia.cats.migrated.v1` stops a second import from that browser.
- **Scenes (E).** `catOfficeFeed.ts` maps cat ids to resident agent ids: `catMessage` (brief, delegate, ask, reply, report) -> work talks; the brief to `team` plays as the boss speaking to one meeting cat; task / final / nudge lines have no second cat and are skipped. Bubble = the message text (wrapped), tooltip = the full text. `flowStateChanged` now carries `rootCatId` and `catIds` (the task's team) for the briefing meeting. The server sends `interrupted` on shutdown; the webview treats it like `error`, and a lost connection ends every open briefing.
- **Queue (F).** Cats whose turn waits for a slot (queued, not running) stand in a line from the coffee spot outward (BFS over walkable tiles), with an "in queue #n" marker. The line tiles go into the reservations like meeting chairs. A talk or meeting takes a cat out of the line; it returns after.
- **Cat console for profile cats (G).** `server/src/catTerminal/officeCatSource.ts` (falls through to the task-board source for other ids). The chat shows the cat's turns across its tasks (in memory, 400 rows, empty after a restart). Send: during a live task the text waits in the cat's inbox (`[Message from the user]`) and its next turn reads it; a cat with no live task gets a new one-cat task in the folder of its newest task. The wheel: only between turns of a live task (no running or queued turn), with the shared `wheel` session lock; turns wait while it is held.
- **Guard.** A delegate / ask to a cat whose engine has no adapter is refused with a tool error (before, the node stayed "working" forever).
- **Flaky test (H).** `catLife.test.ts` seeds the contest roll (no fight) and the idle picks: an idle cat could claim the bed a moment after the forced claim and win a contest fight (about 5 % of runs).
- **E2E (2026-10-06, real CLI, port 5600, real HOME, files restored byte for byte).** Boss Sonnet + two Haiku workers (one with a custom calico coat). Two team tasks through the UI ("create a.txt with 'meow' and b.txt with 'purr', one file per worker", then c.txt / d.txt): residents idle before, briefing meeting with "Briefing the team", delegate and report bubbles, workers typing at their seats, back to idle after, results on the cards, the worker's console shows its turns of both tasks. Task 1: 5 turns, $0.276; task 2: 5 turns, 42 s, $0.246; total $0.52.
- **Known limits.** The task card's "Cat" row still shows the breed name, not the profile name. A sender sometimes speaks from where its approach timed out instead of next to a seated receiver. The narrator keeps the last status line of an idle resident. While the 5600 test server ran, it owned `server.json`, so the hook script reported to it (restored afterwards).

## Resource budget (RAM) — plan to reach the goal cheaper

Measured 2026-10-05 on this Mac: one-shot `claude -p --model haiku` = ~305 MB peak RSS, 15.6 s wall time incl. the model reply. The research measured 300–850 MB per long-lived session.

1. DECIDED (user, 2026-10-05): process per turn, not per cat. Measure the cache-hit rate after `--resume` first. If the cache is lost, fall back to a long-lived process that sleeps after 1 h idle. The prompt cache lives on Anthropic's side, not in the local process. A cat process can exit right after its turn and `--resume` on the next message without losing the cache while the TTL holds. RAM is used only by cats that are working right now. The "sleep after 1 h" rule then only affects the game look, not RAM.
   Cost: a few seconds of startup per turn. Verify the cache-hit rate after a resume in the usage fields before relying on it.
2. Concurrency cap (user, 2026-10-05): at most N cats run a turn at the same time. Default 6. Settings offer 1 to 6 or more. Other cats queue. The game shows them waiting in line, for example at the coffee machine.
3. DECIDED (user, 2026-10-05), replaces the always-on narrator: narrator without a resident process: templates in the server for routine events (read file, run tests, edit). Haiku only for conversation summaries and the task brief, in batched one-shot calls. A long-lived narrator process is not needed.
4. Leaf workers as subagents (optional): a worker that never delegates can run as a subagent inside its parent's process. That uses less RAM, but the user cannot open its own terminal. Off by default.
   Expected peak: about N × 300–850 MB while N cats work, near 0 when the office is idle.

### Narrator decisions (feat/narrator, phase 2c, agent-made 2026-10-06)

- Code: `server/src/narrator/` (templates.ts, schema.ts, haikuBatcher.ts, narrator.ts), contract `core/src/narrator.ts`, webview `webview-ui/src/narratorStore.ts`.
- Input: `NarratorInput` = `{catId, ts, kind: tool|message|state|result, tool?, file?, from?, to?, text?}`. `catId` is the office agent id (number). A `state` event carries `text` = thinking | permission | input | done | error. Today the task board stream-json feeds it (`TaskManagerOptions.narrate`), plus hook-derived `agentToolPermission` / `agentStatus{awaitingInput}` for cats the narrator already speaks for. Phase 1 pushes orchestrator events through `Narrator.push`. Office MCP tools are matched by name suffix (brief, delegate, report, ask, reply, list_team).
- Output: `narratorLine {catId, state, line ≤60}` on change only (a work phase holds 3 s, see below). `narratorSummary {conversationId, catIds, summary ≤120}` from Haiku. Conversation ids: `chat:<sorted from|to>` for messages, `result:<catId>` for results.
- Wire: three WS messages OUTSIDE `core/asyncapi.yaml`: `narratorLine`, `narratorSummary`, `narratorSettings` (server → client), `setNarratorSettings` (client → server). Sent via `store.broadcast` and cast at the webview boundary. **Phase 1 must fold them into asyncapi.yaml** and drop the casts in `narratorStore.ts`.
- Haiku: one call at a time, every 10 s while material is pending, all pending conversations in one call. A failure re-queues and doubles the wait (cap 5 min). A missing `claude` (ENOENT) turns summaries off until restart. Queue caps: 30 conversations, 12 lines each.
- Validator: schema check, then every sentence of a summary must be backed by its input lines: claim words (saved, committed, tests passed, fixed, done …), numbers and file names must appear in the input, checkmarks are always dropped, non-English output (no Latin letters, or any non-Latin letter) is dropped.
- Settings (standalone, per-namespace config): `narratorAiSummaries` (default ON), `narratorRawToolStatus` (default OFF, debug: hover shows the raw "Reading foo.ts"). Settings have no language option.
- Hover: the English line replaces the activity text for non-subagent cats. The last summary shows as a small second line.
- Real call measured 2026-10-06 on this Mac (`npx tsx scripts/narrator-haiku-smoke.ts`, 2 conversations): 4.7 s per call, 1,818 input / 225 output tokens, $0.0029 list price, no cache (the static prompt is below the 4,096-token Haiku cache minimum).
- English prompt, measured 2026-10-06 the same way: 4.6 s per call, 1,700 input / 178 output tokens, $0.0026 list price, no cache. Both English summaries passed the validator.
- **Status lines are abstract work phases in a cat voice (user, 2026-10-06).** No file names, commands or tools in a line. Phase → line: planning (thinking, TodoWrite, unknown tools, `list_team`) → "plotting the hunt"; exploring (Read, Grep, Glob, LS, WebSearch, WebFetch, `ls`/`cat`/`grep`, other MCP tools) → "sniffing around"; building (Edit, Write, NotebookEdit, other shell commands, installs) → "kneading the code"; testing (test, lint, typecheck, build runs) → "batting at bugs"; reviewing (`git status`/`diff`/`log`, `gh pr view`) → "grooming the code"; delivering (commit, push, merge, rebase, `gh pr create`/`merge`, office `report`) → "bringing you a mouse"; office `brief` → "holding a cat meeting"; Task, Agent, office `delegate` → "herding cats"; office `ask`/`reply`, cat messages → "nosing a teammate"; waiting for input (AskUserQuestion, ExitPlanMode) → "meowing for you"; waiting for permission → "pawing at the door"; done, results → "purring, all done"; error → "hissing at a bug". The `state` field keeps its values. A work phase holds at least 3 s (`PHASE_MIN_MS`); a newer one waits, the latest wins. Waiting, done and error show at once. The raw tool status setting still shows the raw text. The Haiku prompt bans file names and commands and allows a light cat tone, still factual.
- **UI language: English only (user, 2026-10-06).** This replaces the earlier Russian narrator decision. Templates, the Haiku prompt and the validator are English, and the social "meow" bubble is English. The Cyrillic font question is closed.

## Team modes — light team is a BACKLOG option (user, 2026-10-05)

Full team is the default. Light team stays in the backlog as a fallback switch, to build if the computer starts to hang (RAM).

- **Full team:** each cat is its own `claude` session (per-turn process, see the RAM plan). Hierarchy of any depth. Each cat has its own terminal in the game.
- **Light team:** only the boss is a `claude` session. Every other cat is a subagent of the boss, with its own prompt, model and skills from its cat profile (generated as subagent definitions). Low RAM: one process. Limits to verify before building: subagent nesting depth, whether a subagent definition can carry its own skills/model/effort, and how subagent events reach the game. A worker has no own terminal in this mode. Its activity shows through the parent session's subagent events (reuse the hidden subagent path from fix/one-cat-per-session: map each subagent to its cat instead of spawning a new character).

## Engine per cat: Claude Code or Codex (user, 2026-10-05)

The cat profile has an engine choice: Claude Code (`claude`) or Codex (`codex`). Model and effort lists depend on the engine and come from the installed CLI, not from memory.
Design rule: the orchestrator talks to cats through an engine adapter (spawn a turn, stream events, resume a session, attach the office MCP tools, open a terminal). Claude Code is the first adapter. Codex is the second.
Verify before building the Codex adapter (from the installed `codex --help` and the docs): headless JSON event stream (`codex exec --json`), session resume, MCP client support for the office tools, the permission/sandbox flags equivalent to skip-permissions, and the interactive resume command for "take the wheel". The narrator maps both event formats to one game event schema.

## Office scenes (feat/office-scenes, phase 2d, agent-made decisions)

- Client interface: `webview-ui/src/orchestratorEvents.ts`, one bus `orchestratorEvents`. Events: `catMessage {from, to, kind: brief|delegate|ask|reply|report, text, summary?}` and `flowStateChanged {taskId, state: briefing|delegating|working|reporting|merging|done|error, bossId, participants}`. Ids are webview agent ids (keys of `OfficeState.characters`): the phase 1 feed maps cat ids to agent ids, then calls `orchestratorEvents.emit(...)`. E2E hook: `__pixelAgentsTestHooks.emitOrchestratorEvent(event)`.
- Director: `engine/officeScenes.ts`. A cat in a scene is a puppet (`engine/scenePuppets.ts`): it skips the character FSM, and CatSocial never puts it in a scene (`isHeld`). Taking a cat drops its idle activity, so the reconcile frees its spot.
- Work talk: the sender walks next to the receiver. A receiver seated at its desk stays seated. Any other receiver stops and turns to the sender. The sender's bubble shows 4 s, then it waits up to 6 s for a reply (any message from the receiver to the sender). Then the sender walks back to its desk and both cats return to the FSM. A queued answer of the receiver joins the talk when it starts. A later reply becomes its own talk.
- Queue: a cat is in one talk at a time. Pending talks start in arrival order per cat; a talk waits while either cat is busy or has an older pending talk.
- Bubble: summary if present, else the text; word-wrapped to 2 lines of 20 chars, "…" when cut. DOM overlay (`components/SceneBubbleOverlay.tsx`) in the pixel font, kind badge in the corner. Two bubbles of a pair grow away from each other. Hover on a bubble or on either cat shows the tooltip with the full summaries of the pair.
- Meeting room: detected at runtime, no layout change (adding an Area to the default layout would only reach new installs without a revision bump). An Area whose label contains "meeting" wins; otherwise the biggest desk-category table with 2 or more chairs next to it. In the default office that is the main room work table (4 chairs, boss head tile above it).
- Meeting: participants sit on the room's chairs (own seat if it is there, else a chair no agent owns). Chairs taken for the meeting go into the reconcile as `seat` reservations, so they outrank a napping cat. Cats without a chair stand next to the table. The boss stands at the head and shows a bubble ("Briefing the team", replaced by the boss's latest `brief` message). Messages between two meeting cats play in place, one talk per cat at a time; messages for a meeting cat from outside wait. When the task leaves `briefing`, everyone walks back to its desk. A new briefing of another task ends the current meeting.
- Known limits: fake hook sessions stay pending (external sessions need confirmation), so the screenshots added agents with the test hooks. A user command (seat / walk) on a cat in a scene is overridden by the scene. A new agent may get a free chair that a meeting holds. A meeting ends only on a non-briefing `flowStateChanged` of the same task (or a new briefing): phase 1 must always send it, also on abort.
